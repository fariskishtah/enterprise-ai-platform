# Support policy — draft placeholder

**Not legal advice. Requires qualified legal, support, and operations review.**

Status: unapproved; service levels, version, and effective date not assigned.

Owners must define eligible contacts, authenticated channels, supported languages
and hours, severity definitions, response/restoration targets, maintenance notices,
status communications, customer diagnostic and security duties, exclusions,
escalation, data-handling rules, and remedies. Targets must reflect staffed on-call
capacity and must not conflict with order forms or incident response.

The production support sender, recipients, and escalation path must be verified.
