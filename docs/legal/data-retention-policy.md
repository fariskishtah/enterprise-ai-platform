# Data retention policy — draft placeholder

**Not legal advice. Requires qualified privacy, legal, security, and records review.**

Status: unapproved; schedule, version, and effective date not assigned.

Owners must approve purpose-based periods and deletion/aggregation for account and
team data, industrial telemetry, uploads/documents, embeddings/indexes, prompts and
conversations, model artifacts/events, monitoring, billing, email/support, audit
logs, security evidence, and backups. Define legal holds, export/return, termination,
backup expiry, deletion verification, exceptions, and accountable system owners.

Published periods must match automated jobs, backup retention, and supplier controls.
